## Spending Limits

> Users can set and adjust their own contactless spending limit directly within the Falcon Finance app.

#### Fees

* **Card issuance:** Free
* **Spending:** Free
* **Currency conversion:** A small FX spread applies when USDf is converted for a transaction in a currency other than USD

## Managing Your Falcon  Card

Within the Falcon Finance app, users can:

* **Freeze or unfreeze** the card at any time
* **View transaction history** for all card activity
* **Set or edit** the contactless spending limit
* **Close the card** and open a new one, if needed
