# Add Falcon Card to Apple Wallet and Google Pay

Falcon Crypto Card can be added to Apple Wallet or Google Pay for convenient contactless payments. Please note that this process is manual — cards must be added by entering your card details, as automatic provisioning is not currently supported.

## Adding to Apple Wallet

1. Open the **Wallet** app on your iPhone.
2. Tap the **+** icon in the top corner to add a new card.
3. Select **Debit or Credit Card**, then tap Continue.
4. Choose Enter Card Details Manually.
5. In the Falcon Finance app, go to your card details section and locate your card number, expiry date, and CVV.
6.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-07-19 at 10.08.48 AM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
7. Enter these details into Apple Wallet as prompted.
8. Complete SMS phone verification step required by Apple Wallet.
9. Once added, your Falcon Card will be available for contactless payments using Apple Pay.



## Adding to Google Pay

1. Open the **Google Pay app or Google Wallet app** on your Android device.
2. Tap the + icon to add a new card.
3. Select **Credit or Debit Card**.
4. Choose the option to Enter Card Details Manually.
5. In the Falcon Finance app, go to your card details section and locate your card number, expiry date, and CVV.
6.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-07-19 at 10.08.48 AM (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
7. Enter these details into Google Pay as prompted.
8. Complete any verification steps required by Google Pay.
9. Once added, your Falcon Crypto Card will be available for contactless payments using Google Pay.
